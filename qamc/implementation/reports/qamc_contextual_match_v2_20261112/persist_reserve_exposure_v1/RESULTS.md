## What changed in answers

- **Board-game gathering, 3776469a.** Reserve receives both the host's cat and a participant's severe allergy, absent from the primary and the equal-count control. Only the reserve proposes a no-cat site and cat-exposure precautions while retaining concrete games, snacks and color accessibility. Qwen grades 2/3/2 for primary/reserve/equal-count and GPT checks all as 3. The reserve still considers the home option and phrases the no-cat site conditionally when severe allergy is already known. Under the stricter predeclared behavior criterion, the primary venue is unresolved. This reproduces the difference in useful evidence since the archived replay, with a real downstream action difference.
- **Birthday dinner, 2af062e3.** Qwen grades 3/3/2. All three keep vegetarian dining, accessibility, a quiet venue and budget. The equal-count answer loses a point for not mentioning the requester's live-music preference. Primary also succeeds without making live music a selection condition. This uneven treatment cannot establish a second causal utility gain or justify elevating the host's taste above the guest's needs.
- **Flowering fence, 7b03625e.** Primary already offers dwarf panicle hydrangea and describes the alkaline-soil challenge. Reserve makes the hydrangea-specific raised-bed route more concrete. The equal-count control also proposes a raised bed or containers. Both graders rate all three 3. The previous archived pass difference does not repeat.
- **Risk checks.** The family dinosaur plaque does not present a private belief as paleontological fact in any arm. The three recruitment pitches remain directed at the requested external audience. No new supported risk failure was observed in this small assignment.

Physical requests were **72**, all OK, including 24 Actor and 48 evaluation calls. Reported tokens **572,455**, no retries and no unknown usage. The wire audit verified identical non-memory envelopes, identical original prefixes, and equal numbers of additions in the two expanded arms for all eight roots. The reserve-minus-control memory block length varied from −28 to +125 characters, so count was matched and exact input length was not. The reserve is based on separately saved selection outputs. An online deployment requiring the second selection would have additional upstream cost. No new selection requests, old-result edits, sealed access or independent human assessments.

The actionable lesson is to keep two metrics separate: whether the evidence reached the generator, and whether it changed the primary decision. Restoring the board-game sources is worth preserving as a case. Another general expansion rule is unsupported by this panel. The current strongest PersistBench and RPEval paths remain the performance anchors.
