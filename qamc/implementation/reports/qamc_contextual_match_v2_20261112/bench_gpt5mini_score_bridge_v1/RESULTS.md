# GPT-5-mini qualification on the fixed BenchPreS audit

The two score-independent qualification requests both ended with HTTP 200 followed by a stream error. No judgment or token usage was received. The 26 other cells stayed undispatched, with zero retries. The alias is not qualified on this gateway. This does not establish general unavailability of GPT-5-mini.

No new Actor, grade change, or sealed access. The existing Strong Gate and Selectivity answers, prior masked Qwen/GPT-4.1 judgments and initial adapted Qwen results remain intact. The paper reported GPT-5-mini human agreement as a validation comparison, but not as the primary DeepSeek-R1 judge. A valid deployment through another provider would remain a separate sensitivity comparison.

The exact originally masked message and sampling settings were used so a successful audit could vary only the model. We did not silently change them after the failures. Offline replay reproduced the two unknown deliveries and the 26 undispatched assignments.
