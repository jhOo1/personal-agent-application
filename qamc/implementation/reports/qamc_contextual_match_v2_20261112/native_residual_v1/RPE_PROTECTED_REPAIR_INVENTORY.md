# RPEval saved-answer repair inventory

This retrospective audit searches for positive behaviors to explain and test. It does not select a new answer online or change any archived grade. All records come from the existing exposed development panel. The reference is the exact IPE-C endpoint. The six comparison methods are the contemporary formal-coverage archive's RP reconstruction, Des2Gen reconstruction, MemAdapter adaptation, and three internal GEPA pipelines. Their histories, prompts and inference costs can differ. All use the recorded project rating protocol.

## Reproducible screen

Join by exact UID. Restrict to inputs with an author B or C and require both strict Match denominators to equal the label count. Mark a potential repair if Match increases and none of OPB, UPB or FM increases. The last screen also requires a distinct answer hash, no recorded diagnostic flag on IPE-C, and no recorded invalid native field on the alternative. The latter two checks have different coverage. A row that passes can still contain a Judge error. Author labels and scores are used only here, after generation.

The opportunity is narrower than another general revision pipeline. A candidate would produce a testable operational consequence of one already applicable condition at a specific decision point, using a trigger and executable replacement while preserving the stated task. A positive comparison needs a matched ordinary Actor, the same real inputs and cost, checks for cases where the first action already works, and checks that a personal preference did not gain authority over other participants. Added choices, mere warnings, failure to verify the trigger, and worsening original constraints should count separately. The two most informative examples are both travel planning, so the signature is not yet evidence of cross-domain improvement. No new scores or generations were obtained.

A stage summary was sent to the designated review chat with these positive and negative controls. The next live decision depends on whether a prospectively selected slice has sufficient eligible cases and an operation meaningfully different from general plan review. Sealed cases remain excluded.
