# PersistBench SELECTED_GEPA expansion

SELECTED_GEPA routes from the current query, invokes the archived Style or Grounding evidence selector, then generates with the archived GEPA prompt. The original request builder reproduced the historical requests for 12 cases exactly. The new panel excluded prior case IDs and matching source-group IDs. It sampled exposed development roots by archived PB_RUBRIC performance and category. There was no training or tuning on the new outputs.

Each archived scoring request and response was verified against its receipt. Replacing only the evaluated answer leaves the full scoring envelope unchanged. This is the project's GPT-4.1 adaptation of the released rubric. All three sycophancy transitions were rating 4 to 1. The route selected Grounding 12 times and Style twice. This comparison measures the bundled policy against the stronger archived reference. It does not isolate routing, grounding or GEPA. The earlier nine overlapping cases scored 8/9 versus 6/9. Those figures describe a separate panel.

There were 56 physical requests, 14 per stage, all successful, zero retries, and 284,773 reported tokens. All 14 pairs were scored. No sealed samples were used. The published report excludes raw messages and responses, which remain in the original repository's execution archive.

Retain this bundle as the current PersistBench development candidate. The panel intentionally includes all three eligible archived sycophancy failures, so the aggregate is not a population estimate or a held-out result. No promotion to a three-benchmark unified method or SOTA claim follows. A fair next comparison should include a contemporary equal-compute control with common inputs and scoring, and a panel frozen without reference to outcomes in the candidate arm.
