# PersistBench identical-signal history execution

This development comparison reuses the verified Grounding/Style receipts for the 14 exposed expansion cases. A nonempty selection gives ON, an empty selection OFF. Both arms receive the same binary signal, identical advisory instruction, and the original PB_RUBRIC generation template. The enforced arm empties the history field on OFF, and the advisory arm leaves the full history visible. The task and generation parameters are identical. ON uses the same full-memory request and one shared output and score by construction.

The five genuinely different scored requests tie at the pass threshold. This includes the three earlier wins over archived PB_RUBRIC. Their gains on this development panel do not require physical removal once the shared OFF instruction is given. The four strict beneficial-use items all route ON, so they offer no evidence about utility under OFF and no independent generation comparison across arms. A sixth OFF comparison lacks an advisory score due to a stream-in-band error with unknown delivery and unknown usage. There were 20 Actor requests and 20 Judge requests, 39 successful transport receipts, one failure, and 345,547 reported tokens. No old Selector, Actor, or score was rerun as part of the old experiment; this new comparison generates fresh paired Actor outputs and uses the pinned project GPT-4.1 rubric adaptation. The sealed data remain untouched.
