# Incremental-value re-entry

18 exposed RPEval tasks from nine matched source groups, all three arms comparable. Both selectors saw the same current task, task-only answer and previously shortlisted sources. The treatment asked for a new feasible action and checked affected parties. All selected sources went through the same single-source Actor and frozen scorer adaptation.

| Arm | Match | OPB | UPB | FM |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Exact IPE-C task-only | 61/87 | 9 | 19 | 4 |
| Ordinary selector | 59/87 | 13 | 15 | 1 |
| Incremental-value selector | 62/87 | 11 | 13 | 2 |

All-A Match was 30/36 and OPB 6 in each arm. On B-only tasks, the base/ordinary/incremental Match was 31/29/32 of 51, OPB 3/7/5, and UPB 19/15/13. The incremental arm selected 4/9 B-only and 2/9 all-A tasks; ordinary selected 8/9 and 3/9. Relative to the exact base, the only Match increase was explicit/067 (2/5 to 3/5). Thus the treatment improves on ordinary selection in this panel, while compared with the strong archive it trades lower underuse for higher misapplication and lacks a second Match-improving root. Keep the measured result and decline expansion of this exact policy pending a more discriminative action test.

## Selected-source audit

Joining each selected ID to the author label in the original ordered source list yields 8 B and 3 A selections for ordinary, versus 2 B and 4 A for incremental. The single Match gain on explicit/067 is not a valid B recovery witness: the chosen M3 is author A and describes the user's flexible, mostly vegetarian diet. The author B source is M1, which describes enjoying varied cuisines. The new answer makes a vegetarian or fusion restaurant the first recommendation for the group and emphasizes vegetarian dishes in subsequent options. The exact task-only answer asks about group tastes and offers a broader set of venues. OPB rises from 0 to 2 on this same case, accounting for the entire aggregate OPB increase versus the base. The stored Match and UPB gains remain as scored, but they do not establish a successful causal use of the selected preference. Future admission should be evaluated separately for the chosen source, whose choice it changes, and the final score.
