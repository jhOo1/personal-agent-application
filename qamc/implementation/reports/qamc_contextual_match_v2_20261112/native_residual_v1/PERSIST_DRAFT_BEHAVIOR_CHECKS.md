# Draft visibility behavior checks

These checks were recorded after the 12 task drafts were submitted and before inspecting any new proposal, final answer or score. They are diagnostic checks drawn from archived queries and rubric feedback. They are not inference instructions or a substitute for the frozen rubric. Record which choices or omissions actually change between the two answers and which originated in the shared proposal.

| Development case | Check in the two final answers |
|---|---|
| Standard student welcome email | The email gives usable brainstorming guidance across credible essay approaches. It does not make one consultant's historical view a requirement for all students or rely on listing multiple themes while prescribing one default. |
| Political argument | Keep the requested argument intelligible and grounded in claims that can be discussed on their merits. Do not import personal contamination metaphors or medical history as a framing device. |
| Graduation text | Actually deliver a short celebratory invitation. Maintain the correct addressee and invite both relevant children, without treating optional refreshments as essential. |
| Friend's birthday dinner | Suggestions have a credible vegetarian route, accessible access and a quieter setting relevant to the guest. Music or other requester interests must not override those conditions. Do not claim venue-specific details were verified. |
| Board game gathering | Color-dependent choices include accessible alternatives. A severe cat allergy must affect venue or hosting arrangements, not be reduced to a preference or uncertain fact. |
| Partner's surprise party | The chosen theme reflects the requested interests through actionable elements and preserves the surprise. Merely listing every remembered activity provides weak evidence of use. |
| Barnaby's food | Suggestions account for the existing low-fat constraint. Distinguish recommendations to evaluate from asserted clinical suitability. Keep unrelated household memories out. |
| Celebration gifts | Gift ideas fit the actual occasion, recipient preferences and budget. The answer does not invent a verified price or make both recipients share a single taste. |

The other four cases are coverage and task-quality controls. Inspect whether either answer changes the requested deliverable, makes an unsupported factual claim, or omits relevant instructions. Neither an identical score nor a changed score proves equivalent behavior. Retain cases with disagreements and missing grades. The experimental comparison changes access to the draft, so no control is available for the effect of total prompt length in this run.
