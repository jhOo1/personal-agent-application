# CAR three-benchmark pilot settlement

**Do not promote CAR.** Its copy-only operation produced distinct complete answers in six cases. It did not clear the frozen criterion of beating both matched controllers with protected benefits across sources. Keep each benchmark's original strong endpoint and retain the experiment as a negative mechanism contrast.

## What the actual operations did

There are eight requested copy programs, six accepted and two exact fallbacks. The accepted copies use four new donors and two archived candidate replies. The remaining 15 valid CAR controllers choose an entire reply. Every final arm had the same task, original sources, candidates and control budget. A literal guard was present in zero of the 23 shared packets. The tested intervention's directly verifiable hard property is preservation of untouched text blocks. On the four scored RPEval copy tasks, CAR and Free each score 10/21 versus 14/21 for IPE-C. The single PersistBench copy ties all controls on native pass. Those copy-only subsets are post hoc diagnostics. [Realized operation counts](PILOT_DELIVERY.json) · [Exposure trace](PILOT_EXPOSURE.json)

Execution-side review used packets that hid method names and numeric scores. The operator had seen some earlier traces. No independent human or calibrated model labels were added. The paired museum case shows the scope error: a personal evening social habit prompts optional night activities in a child's museum outing, while the requested workable museum route receives no corresponding improvement. The driving-vacation copy keeps its original Colorado recommendation but also inserts several unrelated multiday itineraries to carry a food preference. Free applies the optional dinner to an existing stop. The Spanish practice copy realizes more explicit pronunciation practice at answer level, yet its inserted dictionary comes from a prior task-only candidate. A generated donor can miss the exact action that motivates it. These are observed behavior differences and do not override the returned rubric scores.

## Accounting and decision

Physical requests total 205 with 202 successful transports and three endpoint attempts of unknown delivery. Calls by stage are 24 endpoint, 25 opportunity, 23 donor, 69 final, 20 RPEval Judge, 36 BenchPreS Judge and eight PersistBench Judge. Reported use totals 1,279,184 tokens with three unknown counts. There were 32 exact archived logical endpoint reuses and zero reissued failed requests. Two opportunity outputs failed their source contract and never reached the three final arms. The sealed roster remained untouched. A byte-matched terminal replay was completed from preserved requests and receipts using the original workspace, with zero new requests. [Frozen protocol](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md)

Keep paragraph copying as an engineering option for verified mechanical preservation. Retire this generation-and-copy policy as a candidate for improved benchmark performance. The observed errors motivate testing whether an upstream decision can limit **where and how** history has effect. Compare simpler decisions first and require real final-answer evidence before adding labels or spending more calls.
