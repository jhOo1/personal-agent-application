# Decision for RPEval development

Keep IPE-C as the archived position-level comparator. A different whole-answer switch can still improve all-ignore tasks. On the 110 C-bearing tasks, however, even retrospective access to the better of the same two answers would recover only 9 of the 264 positions currently missed. It would be exact on 3 tasks, one mismatch away on 10, and at least two away on 97 across 32 conservative source groups. After symmetrically excluding known grading flags, the corresponding counts are 2, 2 and 63 over 67 tasks. Flags are incomplete diagnostics. Known examples include a family outing whose fully credited answer nevertheless suggests a craft activity against a no-crafts constraint.

Therefore a whole-answer switch over this pool has little room to improve the measured C-bearing outcome. An intervention claiming to solve this weakness must generate materially different answers and preserve multiple applicable and inapplicable decisions in one task. Evaluate full-task and partial Match, actual constraint violations and source-group replication. The observed distance is a count of grader positions, not a count of edits required or verified semantic defects. It does not imply that any one repair can or cannot flip multiple positions. Labels and scores are used only in this offline diagnostic. Results are conditional on the same 274/300 common tasks and the project-adapted automatic grading. No new model calls or independent confirmation.
