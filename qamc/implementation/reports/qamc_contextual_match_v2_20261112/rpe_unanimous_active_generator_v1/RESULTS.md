# Two estimators agree; the delivered sources still include inapplicable history

**Do not promote.** In an exploratory zero-call replay on 300 exposed RPEval tasks, the frozen rule selected Des2Gen on eight of 137 history-on tasks. Seven switches had paired grades. There were two wins, five ties, zero losses; the eighth lacked a reference Match score. Both wins came from the same dining-room source group and had the same query. Each gained one position. On the seven scored switches the total changed from 23/41 to 25/41.

Across the 282 paired tasks Match changed from 970/1510 to 972/1510, OPB from 257 to 258, UPB from 266 to 262 and FM from 53 to 50. On 220 pairs free of the archived grade flags, Match changed from 818/1213 to 820/1213. The small gain fell below the frozen two-source requirement and worsened overuse severity. The sample is not independent confirmation.

Offline inspection found that **all eight triggered tasks have at least one author A**, despite both estimators predicting that every source applied. Five of eight have the identical query about furnishing a dining area after moving in with a partner. The two improved answers are implicit/119 and implicit/139, both 2/6 to 3/6. Explicit/048 and explicit/116 tied. Explicit/134 lacked the original Match grade. The remaining three tasks had different queries and all tied. Agreement was strongly concentrated by task and failed as a certificate of universal applicability. No threshold search was run. Cold deployment would still incur the reader and generator costs.
