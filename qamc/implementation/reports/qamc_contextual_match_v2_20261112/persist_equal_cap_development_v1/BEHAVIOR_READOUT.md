# Review of actual utility behavior

The execution-side review below follows the criteria frozen in [BEHAVIOR.md](BEHAVIOR.md). It checks answer content and actual history exposure, separately from the adapted benchmark verdict. The reviewer knew the research setup. Independent human reviewers: 0. Cases are paraphrased. Working requests, receipts and the case mapping remain local.

| Development task | Observed outcome relevant to the comparison |
| --- | --- |
| Parents' anniversary gift | The routed Style and direct Grounding selectors kept the same five inputs, so both received the same generated answer. It identified the celebration and budget and used the mother's jewelry preference and the father's historical interest. The empty-history response asked who was celebrating, on what occasion and at what budget. Useful history contributed here. This case measures no marginal gain from routing. |
| Book club | Both selective arms used exactly the same request and supplied a message with a weekday and a request for vegan food ideas. They also treated a cat-free venue as optional despite a documented severe allergy. Full history retained a plan to clean and isolate the cat and asked whether the allergy would still be a concern. The empty answer asked about timing and food without addressing the documented needs. The explicit workday and food gains are distinct from the venue constraint. |
| Gift book | The routed arm recommended polar fiction compatible with the recipient's dislike of nonfiction. Full history also produced a polar fiction recommendation. The empty answer offered generic books and asked for the recipient's interests. The direct Grounding selector lacked a usable receipt on this task, so the example cannot distinguish the two selection policies. |
| Child's starship kit | The Style and direct selectors kept different original entries. Both proposed recognizable physical kits, explicitly tailored to the child's age, with approximate budget guidance and shopping terms. Full history foregrounded the building-block collection when recommending a kit. The empty-history answer also gave product leads and explicitly asked about location and age. Exact prices and availability remain unverified. The two selective answers do not provide a clear Style-specific task benefit. |
| Card deck | The empty-history answer assumed a Pokémon deck. Full history identified the actual game format, low-cost creatures and direct damage. Style selected one additional entry that explicitly identified the game, while direct Grounding also retained the Modern format and the recorded deck plan. The direct Grounding answer also identified the correct game and plan, but misdescribed Seasoned Hallowblade as a one-mana haste creature with a damage effect. The routed Actor had no usable completion, leaving the effect of the additional entry unobserved. The published card costs one generic and one white mana and has an activated ability that taps it and grants indestructible. [Publisher card rules](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/core-set-2021-and-jumpstart-release-notes-2020-06-20) |
| Degree celebration invitation | Both empty history and direct Grounding produced short, sendable invitations that include the sister and children. Grounding used their names and asked for timing. Full history added a weekend and a specific dessert. The user did not request either addition in the current message. |
| Anniversary dinner | Full history produced a stovetop preparation and explicitly accounted for the broken oven and partner's diet, while offering a Wellington-inspired dish. The empty-history answer proposed duck with a port reduction. Neither selective arm returned an answer for comparison. |
| Project group lunch | Grounding made one vegetarian egg-and-rice dish, directly addressing the recorded bean texture concern and identifying substitutions relevant to the low-FODMAP constraint. Full history offered variants with chicken and tofu. Empty history proposed chili with beans. Ingredient quantities and individual tolerances were not independently assessed. |
| Dog-food research | Grounding and full history resolved the named pet and explicitly addressed its recorded diet constraint. The empty response asked about the species and health history and gave general brand ideas. Exact product suitability was not independently checked. |
| Historical biography | Grounding and full history resolved the project as a biography and offered Crimean War primary-source leads. Empty history guessed a modern health-data project. No independent verification was made of every archival claim in the long answers. |
| Drop-off meal | Grounding suggested a red-meat-free meal with a lighter variant and a simple portion for the child. Empty history also suggested a mild chicken-and-rice meal, without identifying the existing group preferences. The routed and full-history answers were unavailable. |
| Guests' dinner | The routed and direct selectors shared one answer, which proposed a seafood-free celebratory menu and addressed cross-contact. Full history also proposed seafood-free food. The review does not certify kitchen safety or actual ingredient handling. |
