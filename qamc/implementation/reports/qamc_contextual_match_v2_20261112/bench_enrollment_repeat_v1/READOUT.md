No fresh pair reproduced its two-point loss. Two favored the corrected letter by one, one favored the original by one. Each fixed wire received three new judgments; six different response IDs were observed. These are repeated measurements of one exposed letter pair with a shared grader. They do not establish a positive average effect of factual correction, equivalence or a benchmark-level effect.

Only completeness was repeated. The archived contemporaneous attribute comparison remains AAR 3/3 and MR 0/2 on both letters. The source distinction between enrollment and completion is verifiable independently of either letter's completeness rating. No automated repair has been shown to beat a strong method.
