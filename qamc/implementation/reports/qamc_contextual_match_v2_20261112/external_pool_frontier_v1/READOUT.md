# Can saved full-method answers supply the missing alternatives?

The roster is the same 92 exposed development tasks as the preceding revision-pool audit. RP, Strong Gate, MemAdapter and Des2Gen are checked on available RPEval items. BenchPreS also includes adapted Selectivity. PersistBench compares Strong Gate, RP, PB_RUBRIC, PB_GEPA and PB_NO_MEMORY. Methods have different generation cost. We use the frozen project grading adaptations and require aligned evaluation denominators. The complete comparison covers observed outputs only; an exact-text scoring conflict is excluded. No new generation or grading.

This calculation allows an oracle to select the incumbent when no alternative improves it. External gains in RPEval also survive the archived Match/bias/ordinal flag check. The birthday-party case alone accounts for seven positions. Des2Gen supplies one position each on a family driving itinerary and a dinner request; RP and Gate also each supply two positions on the Qingdao trip. These are score differences on already exposed sources; they do not establish unique behavioral repairs without further examination.

Fixed deployment gives a different answer. On 24 RPEval tasks RP scores 70/130 and IPE-C 66/130; on this particular roster Strong Gate ties IPE-C at 66/130. On 24 common BenchPreS tasks MemAdapter realizes 23/40 appropriate uses versus Strong Gate's 34/40, even though the annual-physical message supplies a protected improvement. On 42 common PersistBench tasks always omitting memory passes 25 and PB_RUBRIC passes 38. Three answer-selection opportunities relative to PB_RUBRIC do not show that a new method beats SELECTED_GEPA. Other denominators and baselines are in SUMMARY.json.

For BenchPreS this establishes a concrete but narrow alternative and identifies the cost of adopting its generator wholesale. The proposed next optimization targets direct Strong Gate answer generation with its exact selected evidence fixed. Compare source-group-separated development fits with the same opportunity for score-only versus behavioral feedback and a fresh unmodified Actor control. This remains a proposed experiment.
