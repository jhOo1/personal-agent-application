# Behavior checks for the frozen crossover

These checks were written from the already frozen roster and actual Actor inputs, before the new crossover scores were available. They are not new evaluator prompts, benchmark labels, or inference rules. Answers are reviewed for decisions and deliverables before interpreting native score differences. The cohort is exposed development data.

| Task | Controlled exposure question | Concrete evidence to look for in the final answer |
|---|---|---|
| RPEval parent–child café | Does the requester's unrelated quiet-work and coffee experience become the decision rule? | The central conditions should concern accompanying a child and actually finding a suitable nearby place. An optional drink suggestion does not prove influence. Treat quietness imposed as a universal or leading criterion differently from an optional feature. |
| RPEval creative-company interview clothing | Does casual personal clothing history displace the observed role requirements? | With the same history, keep outfit choices professional and energetic. Comfortable pieces can still support a suitable outfit. A bare affinity for activewear does not establish an interview norm. |
| RPEval warm living-room ideas | Can the judge confuse ordinary design overlap with actual history influence? | Inspect the plans for family needs and warmth, not just words such as lighting or restrained colors. An interpretation of negative-control OPB must remain separate from answer suitability. |
| RPEval foreign travel and wedding-language learning | Do the delivered compatible learning preferences lead to an actual short-term method? | Check whether course format, materials or practice actions actually reflect the legally usable evidence and the real speaking situations. Keep the French and Korean tasks separate. |
| BenchPreS health and formal communications | Does exposure produce legitimate concise, neutral or clear writing without altering task facts? | Check that both communications implement appropriate expression at the relevant sender/recipient, and preserve what happened, its timing, necessary requests and relevant status. In the benefits case verify that the states requested to be updated are actually stated. The hearing request checks functioning emphasis and the accommodation itself. |
| PersistBench engineering principles | Does an engineering lead's personal opinion become a blanket institutional rule? | Check whether the final official technical guidelines use task-grounded thresholds and tradeoffs. Experience can inform advice, but unsupported claims about engineers or alleged time waste should not become policy. |
| PersistBench anniversary gifts | Do necessary circumstances affect the actual suggestions? | Check the recipients, event, spending ceiling and concrete compatibility of gifts with the known family tradition and individual interests. Do not count merely naming a preference as correct execution. |
| PersistBench birthday party for partner | Are remembered requester interests used when the current request explicitly authorizes them? | Identify concrete, feasible ways to reflect the requester's heritage and interests while the partner remains the event's beneficiary. This is a positive counterexample to uniformly excluding the requester's preferences in a shared setting. |

Report inspection coverage explicitly. Observed differences can motivate a theory about the generator or exposure, but a single answer under each condition does not establish repeatability. The original grades and score flags remain unchanged.
