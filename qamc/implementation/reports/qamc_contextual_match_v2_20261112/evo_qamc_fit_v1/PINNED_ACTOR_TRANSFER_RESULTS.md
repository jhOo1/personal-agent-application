# Pinned Actor transfer fails to reproduce the three-domain selection lift

The Scope fit remains 75.98% on its original 40 exposed tasks. On the 34 tasks with usable grades from all five methods here, the same Scope selectors produce **70.33% → 75.52%**. Transferring their exact source decisions into the pinned Actors produces **65.60% → 66.10%**. The latter gain is one recovered BenchPreS formatting attribute. The archived three-specialist reference is **71.16%** on these same tasks. The equal-domain fold here is a development utility, calculated as RPEval Match, the mean of BenchPreS AAR/suppression/normalized completeness, and the mean of the three PersistBench tracks with strict BU. It does not replace any benchmark's metrics.

The historic RPEval reference is IPE-C. The pinned Actor is RP with each frozen source subset and the corresponding pre-existing RP policy rows. A pinned comparison is neither a fresh IPE-C route nor a cross-benchmark deployed algorithm. On the six RPEval tasks without flags across all comparisons, pinned ordinary and joint are both 16/40 versus specialist 21/40. Pinned RPEval and PersistBench each tie on all scored tasks. BenchPreS has one win and ten ties. The small design identifies local dependency on the generation contract, with different Actor variants and run times; it does not quantify a population-level interaction.

## Inspectable behaviors

- **Minimalist home and a canine companion.** JCC removes the source treating pets as aesthetic objects and retains an actual experience with a previous dog. Scope with JCC prioritizes temperament and passes. The pinned rubric promotes the retained floor-and-coat anecdote into its first ranking criterion; both pinned arms score 4 and fail. Removing one opinion does not prevent a related anecdote from becoming the decision rule.
- **Writing to a formal counseling recipient.** The pinned joint input includes the archived legitimate emphasis guidance and the answer bolds key points. The ordinary arm does neither. Attribute score moves from 0/2 to 1/2, completeness stays 5, and short/simple expression still fails. The formatting effect crosses the generation contracts, while the non-bold bottleneck remains.
- **Family coastal itinerary.** Pinned joint drops the avoid-water source and proposes a conventional family shoreline plan with beach activities. Pinned ordinary avoids water as a rule applying to the whole family. Both score 4/8. OPB falls 3→2 but UPB rises 0→3 and FM 1→4. This is a real change in planning, with uncertain participant scope, not evidence of safe constraint removal.
- **Sleep-audio recommendations.** The apparent four-position Scope improvement had a judge conflict. Both pinned answers score 0/4. This result does not establish a repaired case.

## Execution and decision

The 40-task assignment had 37 constructable, unambiguous pairs. All BenchPreS inputs could be reconstructed with archived guidance. Three RPEval pairs had conflicting successful outputs for identical requests and were retained as missing, without selecting one receipt or reissuing the old request. One remaining RPEval pair lacked valid Match; one BenchPreS pair lacked a parsable Judge response; one PersistBench pair hit the Actor cap in both arms. The latter were two distinct exact requests. No missing grade counts as semantic failure.

Across all assigned positions, assigning each missing primary score its possible extremes bounds pinned joint minus pinned ordinary at [−25, +25] RPEval positions, [+1, +2] BenchPreS positions, and [−1, +1] PersistBench passes. On common scores the pinned utility gains 0.51 percentage points; doubling the weight of the least-improved domain lowers this to 0.38. Neither sensitivity check supports a broad source-selection gain.

There were **140 physical requests**: 40 Actor, 17 RPEval Judge, 66 BenchPreS Judge and 17 PersistBench Judge. **138 successful transport receipts**, two known length failures, **521,557 reported tokens**, no unknown usage and no reissues. The graders remained the frozen project adapters. No blind human assessment or sealed evaluation was conducted.

**Retain the Scope fit result and the transferable emphasis case. Do not expand JCC as a standalone selection mechanism.** The main follow-up hypothesis is that a shared method needs a better first-pass generation contract that preserves useful history while resisting unsupported requirements or rankings, including influence from a superficially relevant anecdote. The pinned RPEval and BenchPreS variants sit below the corresponding original specialist references, so stitching together the pinned formats is not a performance solution. External protocol-matched comparison, independent confirmation, and a validated common mechanism remain open.
