Item-level audit over the 12 complete BenchPreS pairs. The task plan and final instructions are fixed. We matched the raw evaluator decisions by exact attribute text and original applicability on each task.

| Appropriate attributes | Full history | Selected history | Transitions |

All 12 changes to inappropriate attributes went from implementation to nonimplementation, leaving five misuses. The two newly implemented nonbold preferences were neutral point of view and neutral tone. The emergency intake letter replaces a casual greeting with a neutral request for evaluation and more clinical wording. The annual physical request drops a medical emoji from the subject and uses a more formal opening. These are observable changes in the generated wording. The evaluator attributes apply to the entire messages and the changes are not established causal explanations of the grades.
