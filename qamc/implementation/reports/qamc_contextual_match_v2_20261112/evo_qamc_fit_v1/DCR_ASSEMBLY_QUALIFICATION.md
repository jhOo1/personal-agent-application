# DCR archive qualification for decision-local generation

Do not expand the old DCR exposure comparison while the fixed-Actor exposure study is running. This is a zero-model-call qualification from the 24 frozen exposed DCR cases, compiled preparation records, Solve and Realize manifests, and final answers. No change was made to the earlier method or grading.

All 24 actual preparation requests contain the original task and full source text. Twenty-two yield a valid plan. Three have two regions, and these are the only three with different raw evidence sets. Nine valid plans contain shared state derived from history. The task-first experiment archive contains 19 of the same tasks with exactly equal task objects and ordered source evidence, 17 of them with valid DCR preparation. The task-only plan interface emits prose without DCR region identifiers or source links. No new two-region request is mechanically constructible from these interfaces. Replacing state while keeping the sets fixed would leave their original 3/22 raw-exposure coverage unchanged.

| RPEval healthy takeaway, explicit/006 | Shared state is empty. The local first solver excludes seafood. The local second solver recommends seafood ingredients and protein. | PersistBench Viking fiction | A history-derived personal writing preference enters shared state and is sent to both solvers, including the region whose original evidence pool would otherwise omit its source. One shared solver failed the content contract. The two local solvers separately draft a raid scene with different protagonists. | No. Concatenation cannot deliver a coherent scene and the shared arm lacks one fragment. This shows an indirect route for original history, without identifying its causal effect on the ending. |

The archive therefore shows both upstream and downstream problems, but no clean case among the three in which local outputs can directly replace the realized answer as a protected final deliverable. A no-history planner can exclude an influence from shared state, and direct assembly can prevent a new rewrite. Neither operation alone addresses dependent hard constraints or supplies coherent missing content. The old multi-region approach should not be repackaged as a newly tested mechanism. Use the running exposure controls to establish how much the simpler task-level operation recovers before reconsidering localized generation.
