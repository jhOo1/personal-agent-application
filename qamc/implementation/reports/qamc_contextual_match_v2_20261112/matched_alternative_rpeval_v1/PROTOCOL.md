# Matched alternatives before choosing how to use memory

The intervention changes what candidates are available to a complete-answer editor. Prior candidate-distribution work asked the model to generate a whole answer and choose its own realization in the same call. Here there is no proposal-stage answer, scoring, baseline draft or adoption decision. An upstream call supplies two actual alternative actions. A second common Actor realizes the whole deliverable after seeing the suggestions and the frozen strong draft. The first prompt is the only method-specific system instruction. Both arms use the same current task, original unmodified history and facts at both stages.

- OPEN supplies two meaningfully different concrete ideas, with source use left to ordinary judgment.
- MATCHED supplies two concrete plans for the same consequential choice under the same present conditions, recipients, purpose and hard requirements. First propose a task-sufficient option. The second can add a legitimately useful detail where the holder of a historical preference can benefit without imposing it on someone else. If none qualifies, provide another useful task-only alternative. This is a means to generate competition without committing to use history, not a newly enforced semantic contract.

Both supply arms have two equally bounded records and no external data. A records-only syntax contract rejects invalid or exactly duplicated ideas; it does not establish feasibility, independence, specificity or correct scope. Both final Actors receive the same message fields, identical original bytes and identical system prompt. They may use, combine or ignore suggestions. They may keep the entire strong answer. No Critic, extra choice picker or hard rendering constraint is added. No real-world availability checks are claimed for generated venues, products or other items. The original benchmark preferences and authors' labels never enter inference.

The development panel uses all six B-only groups of the frozen exposed candidate-distribution comparison, plus three A-only and three C-containing groups selected by a fixed hash, and the single-preference V1 parents' brunch witness separately. Choosing a subgroup reads stratum only; prior results are used as baselines, not to pick within each stratum. Baseline answers come from exact IPE-C and V1 archives. Multi grading uses the archived GPT-4.1 RPEval request and single grading the Qwen adapted single contract, preserving the same case and score aliases. Keep the single case separate. Multi results are compared on exact common questions with IPE-C, RP reconstruction, Strong Gate and the historical candidates where compatible scores exist. These development groups are exposed and revisited intentionally for a mechanistic contrast, with no independent confirmation or official parity implied.

Report the complete assigned denominator, successful candidate and answer counts, exact reuse, native Match/OPB/UPB/FM, exposure failures and real cost. Mark invalid first-stage contracts separately, carry forward the original answer for an invalid structured response, and keep delivery failures missing. No repeat calls after unknown delivery. Each arm has at most two inference calls per case and one grade. Scoring of final variants happens only after final answers are saved. To advance, require at least two separate B source groups with a concrete task-compatible gain in the actual answer that OPEN lacks, positive B Match change versus both IPE-C and OPEN, and no overall A/C Match, OPB or FM deterioration relative to IPE-C. The already-known brunch case cannot meet the independent-groups requirement. Use executed answers, not proposal enthusiasm, to decide.
