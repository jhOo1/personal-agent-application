# Identical-wire score replication reverses the apparent completeness effect

All six previously edited messages entered once per arm for completeness. Both cases with a concise/brief preference entered once per arm for that attribute. Prior inspection of the common experiment tree found no additional matching hash in its standard single-stage visible record directories. New draws used the exact stored grader request, including the answer, model alias, settings and instructions. The original grade remains authoritative for its original study; this is a separate measurement diagnostic. The initially observed answers and score changes motivated this panel.

| Paired edit minus original | First grading | Replication | Identical-wire grades that changed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Completeness total over six | −2 | +2 | 4/12 |
| Following brevity, two cases | +1 | 0 | 1/4 |

The civil dispute letter's brevity gain did not recur. One task's completeness contrast reversed from −1 to +1 and another changed from −1 to 0. These results demonstrate variability for the same observable requests, without identifying why the gateway or grader varied. They do not estimate population reliability, prove that the edits are equivalent, or warrant choosing the more favorable draw. The other attributes were not regraded. Retain Strong Gate; there is no reproducible benefit of removing the duplicate recipient line on this evidence.
