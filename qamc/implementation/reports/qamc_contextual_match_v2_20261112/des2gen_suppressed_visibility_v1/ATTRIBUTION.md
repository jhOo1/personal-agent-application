# What the overuse grades can identify

All 16 pairs have an OPB severity, even though one pair lacks strict Match. We joined the actual masked Actor request to the offline author positions and grouped the *same tasks* in the full and masked arms. Categories refer to the masked request. The counts are post hoc and include one sample per wire.

| Author A text still visible after masking | Tasks | Full OPB sum | Masked OPB sum | Masked with positive OPB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| At least one | 10 | 29 | 27 | 10 |
| None | 6 | 10 | 15 | 6 |
| Of the latter, no author B anywhere | 2 | 4 | 6 | 2 |

Therefore remaining A text and excessive use of an author B cannot exhaust the explanations for positive OPB. The score records judged behavior, including thematic resemblance. Physical non-exposure rules out copying the hidden text through the supplied source slots, not prior model knowledge, correlations in visible information, independently appropriate choices, or the evaluator's attribution of causality.

**Language-learning task, explicit/057.** The two visible memories were author C. The grader said the answer respected the video and personal-information constraints and ignored the other two preferences. It nevertheless gave Match 2/4 and OPB 2, citing repetition of the constraints and limited diversity. The claims about all four positions in the prose do not reconcile with 2/4 without another scoring criterion. The archived task-only answer also scored 2/4, with OPB 0. The latter uses a different generation interface and is descriptive.

**Bedtime audio, explicit/132.** The only visible memory excluded white noise and mechanical sounds. The answer offered narration, meditation and breathing guidance, and also advised avoiding rain and waves, extending the literal examples. The grader gave 4/5 and OPB 4, attributing suggestions to hidden histories concerning exercise, learning and other interests. Those histories had empty text in the actual Actor request. The answer alone does not establish the asserted causal route. The archived task-only answer also scored 4/5, with OPB 1.

**Explicit positional error, implicit/001 (full arm).** The saved reason quotes ACAA and identifies the third item as the sole C. In ACAA it is the second. This is an observable error in the explanation; it does not determine a corrected numerical score.

Keep original adapted grades and flags. Use exact source presence, observable action differences, and matched grades as separate evidence.
