# Does replacing an ignored source with a supporting source change the delivered action?

We reconstructed all one-for-one A-to-B exchanges in the two count-matched evidence arms. Author letters were joined retrospectively to the saved selector IDs. Two of the 12 allocated roots qualify; both are family trips. Two saved draws per arm give eight answers, with their original detailed project GPT-4.1 judgments. Four comparisons are repeated measurements on two tasks.

- Xi'an 073: the transferred policy replaces position 3 (A, evening relaxation) with position 6 (B, nature). Both transferred answers propose open parks/wetlands and avoid long queues; neither schedules a night-view outing. Both RP count-matched answers also propose parks and avoiding queues, and add a city-night outing. Thus an actual action changes twice, but the supported outdoor activity was already in the comparator. Match is 5/9 versus 6/9 in both draws; OPB is 1 for both arms and UPB is 2 versus 1 in both draws.
- The first Xi'an transferred Judge rationale specifically says position 3 should be partially used. The author string AAACBBAAA has A at position 3. The second rationale treats ignoring night interests as consistent with A, awards the same total, and criticizes weak personalization of food recommendations. Neither archived result has an automated flag. This is an identifiable rationale inconsistency on the first draw. The aggregate Match does not identify which individual positions were credited. No corrected score is inferred.
- Chengdu 000: position 1 (A) is replaced with position 7 (B). Both RP answers allow quiet night activities at the accommodation, including stargazing. Both transferred and RP answers keep low-crowd family activities. A passing mention of avoiding night crowds in the second transferred answer illustrates why word presence cannot identify a recommended activity. All four answers score 6/9. The first draw trades one less OPB for one more UPB; on the second these severities tie.

This audit identifies one repeatable action difference and no Match gain. The two swaps change both which A is absent and which B is present, so the eight answers do not isolate their separate marginal effects. Do not rerun the same arms or infer a new generator benefit.
