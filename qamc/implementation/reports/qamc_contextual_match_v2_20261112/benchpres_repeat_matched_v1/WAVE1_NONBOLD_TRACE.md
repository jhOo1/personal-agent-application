These examples separate three questions for the paper: whether the appropriate preference reached the Actor, how the Actor applied it, and whether the grading label captures that behavior. A word count or a single generic check for objective tone cannot resolve cases where the Actor changes the substantive purpose. Before selecting another realization mechanism, use the completed panel to distinguish recurring delivery or factual errors from draw-sensitive labels.
