# Concise answer choice improves expression counts but loses completeness

Frozen two-answer retrospective deployment on existing development drafts and adapted Qwen grades. 48 assigned, 43 with original wires, 41 with a delivered baseline, 39 with both outputs and complete grades. All comparisons use those same 39. The same-pool controls bear the same two-answer cold-start generation cost, while the incumbent needs only its original answer. New requests: zero. Sealed cases unaccessed.

| Selection rule | Alternate uses | AAR | Non-bold AAR | MR | Completeness total /39 |

| Strong Gate fresh answer | 0 | 60/70 | 38/48 | 1/125 | 182 |
| Always numeric-feedback alternate | 39 | 60/70 | 39/48 | 0/125 | 155 |
| Shorter when brevity guidance present | 6 | 62/70 | 40/48 | 1/125 | 175 |
| Same plus numeric/format protection | 4 | 61/70 | 39/48 | 1/125 | 178 |

The four guarded changes spanned three profiles. The civil dispute message gained one appropriate implementation, but both original and alternate messages had placeholders and completeness 3. Annual physical remained 2 appropriate implementations and completeness 5. The tax notice and rental history each retained their original appropriate count but fell from completeness 4 to 2. The tax alternate requested review and advice, while the original also explicitly sought written confirmation. The housing original included mortgage payment history; the alternate devoted space to placeholders for prior rental details. These are observable differences, not verified explanations of individual judge points. Numeric equality does not preserve actions, numbers expressed as words, or the relationship of an amount to its subject. The brevity cue may also be an instruction for simple delivery rather than an explicitly stated user preference.

Decision: retain Strong Gate. No rule tuning on these 39 pairs. The single gain demonstrates candidate-level opportunity, not stable net benefit. Missing scores have not been assigned zeros. No independent validation or human ratings.
