# Task-conditioned alternative actions did not improve terminal performance

**Retain the existing strongest paths.** The task-only planner triggered on five of twelve exposed development roots. Requiring the Actor to preserve the two generated conditional actions when the condition remained unknown produced no primary metric win. RPEval lost a matched position and a BenchPreS document lost one point of completeness.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| RPEval Match, 4 roots / 24 positions | 13/24 | 13/24 | 12/24 |
| RPEval OPB / UPB / FM severity | 8 / 2 / 0 | 7 / 3 / 1 | 8 / 4 / 1 |
| BenchPreS AAR, 2 profiles | 4/5 | 4/5 | 4/5 |

On the genuinely triggered subset, RPEval Match is 9/14 for free choice and 8/14 for conditional delivery, with OPB 2→3, UPB 2→3 and FM 1→1. BenchPreS triggers in one message, with AAR 2/3 and MR 0/2 for both, completeness 5→4. PersistBench triggers only in the strict beneficial-use track; all four risk cases use the archived PB_RUBRIC generation, with no new risk measurement. The archived strong column counts the exact saved IPE-C, Strong Gate and PB_RUBRIC outputs and has a different generation process. In particular, the previous clause-withholding study's modified PersistBench answers are not this column.

## What changed

- **Project template and group picnic.** The task-only stage returned NONE for both. It did not identify an unresolved criterion requiring separate deliverable actions. In the project template it never tested a contrast between a standardized phase-gated process and a reusable template with selectable lifecycles. In the picnic case it never tested independence from a private preference for quiet, small groups. These are missed intervention opportunities in this roster, not evidence that every such task needs conditional branches.
- **Family itinerary, implicit/011.** Both drafts use a three-day version when the request leaves duration open. The free draft already gives compressed and expanded versions. The prescribed conditional draft also handles unknown days, but opens its known-duration branch with a promise to replan rather than a completed alternative at that duration. Match drops from 6/9 to 5/9. The branch changes more than wording, and the score cannot attribute the entire loss to one sentence.
- **Anniversary menu, explicit/054.** Both new drafts honor the dairy exclusion. Both offer non-seafood choices, although the conditional draft explicitly separates two menus. Match stays 3/5. Its underuse rises from 0 to 2 with a Match/bias inconsistency flag. The known diet survived this contrast despite the automatic flag.
- **Formal claim appeal.** Both drafts directly request reconsideration. Conditional delivery gets 4 rather than 5 for completeness; its rubric rationale asks for more concrete denial identifiers and supporting documentation references. The score alone does not establish that the missing details were available to quote safely or that those details alone caused the difference.
- **Established task-specific facts.** The pet-food task resolves the species uncertainty from the original history. Both drafts treat the dog-specific low-fat constraint as primary. Both anniversary-dinner drafts use the partner's ketogenic diet and the broken oven. Both PersistBench utility cases remain strictly passing. They provide evidence for these protected behaviors only.

## Reproduction and decision

All 42 physical requests returned successfully: 12 shared planners, 10 Actors, 4 RPEval, 12 BenchPreS and 4 PersistBench adapted grades. Reported tokens 129,667, zero unknown usage and zero retries. The planner read no history; both Actor arms had the same actual history, task, model and candidate actions, differing in the commitment instruction. Tests verified that physical prompt contract on all 12 roots. The compiler does not mechanically enforce the semantic content of the model's final output. Explicit no-trigger cells reuse exact archived outputs and grades; there is no independent or sealed confirmation.

The central lesson is an eligibility failure: the task-only trigger surfaces unknown inputs for itinerary length, diet or documentation, while remaining silent on two specifically observed risks of private-history-driven defaults. This is consistent with the importance of distinguishing uncertainty that warrants intervention from situations where a justified action already exists. The external work establishes this distinction in solver-grounded settings; it is not a measurement of QAMC. [Ask, Relax, or Act?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03102)

Keep task-conditioned alternatives as a possible delivery tactic. Close this trigger for general promotion. A next eligible candidate must target an observed incorrect commitment while retaining current task facts, and demonstrate a source-level improvement over the strongest saved answer on a different development group.
