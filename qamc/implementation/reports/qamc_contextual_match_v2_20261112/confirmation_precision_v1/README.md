# What an independent beneficial-use screen could establish

The earlier public-source census in the private research checkout retained 117 cross-domain rows (110 groups), 141 sycophancy rows (131 groups) and 12 strict beneficial-use rows (12 groups) after its lexical exposure screening. These are historical ceilings within that census. Current eligibility and semantic independence remain unverified. All 24 entries of an earlier reserve already matched development or registered inputs; none can be presumed independent. The source report fingerprints accompany this note. No confirmation inputs, IDs, answers, labels, or new split were opened or exported.

Even if all 12 beneficial-use groups remained eligible, all answered and scored reliably, and none showed a paired loss against the comparator, a one-sided exact 95% upper confidence limit on the probability of a paired loss would be 22.1%. A paired loss means the comparator passes strict beneficial use and the proposed method fails on the same root. The calculation assumes independent representative draws with a common loss probability. It is a hypothetical precision calculation, not an estimate on this finite convenience sample. Under those assumptions, a zero-loss screen requires at least 29 independent groups to put the upper limit below 10%, and 59 below 5%. These are illustrative tolerances, not preregistered margins. Net noninferiority is a distinct estimand that also credits wins. Multiple deployments of a root do not increase the number of independent roots.

The historical RPEval inventory also placed the multi-preference explicit and implicit tracks at at most seven unobserved rows and two connected groups apiece; cross-track overlap prevents adding these counts without a new source audit. For BenchPreS the prior inventory included all ten profiles and all 39 query contexts; unobserved combinations can test recombination within known profiles. The pinned RPEval public short judge omits Match. The project has no verified official PersistBench provider receipt or qualified BenchPreS DeepSeek-R1 scorer in the manuscript's comparison record.

**Decision.** A plan that depends on certifying a low paired-loss rate on unseen PersistBench beneficial-use sources cannot be justified from this inventory. Keep independent risk outcomes and positive-use counts separate. Report all known-source utility outcomes, missing pairs, and actual behavior alongside any future source-disjoint risk result. Any later evaluation on a refreshed subset should describe the finite evaluated cases and the true source count. No substitute benchmark or test dispatch follows from this calculation.
