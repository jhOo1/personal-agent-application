# Same-task terminal generation under a crossed nested-history diagnostic

The archived natural nested RPEval pairs keep the same public task and differ in original
history. Earlier nested experiments used these pairs to compare selectors or existing
answers. In this trial, run the same Qwen Actor and scoring envelope on the expanded
task across a two-by-two exposure design. Include or omit the shared historical records
and the records appearing only in the expanded history. STRIPPED shows none, SHARED
shows only the intersection, ADDED shows only the nested difference, FULL shows both.
Within each cell preserve the ordering of the expanded native history. The Actor sees
only the original task fields, raw exposed source records and the shared generic
instructions. It receives no author label, reference answer, old score, case ID or
annotated policy. The grader reads the complete public benchmark evidence for the
expanded task in every cell.

Select three distinct exposed source groups from the previously verified soft-only
nested roster and three groups with added hard-constraint annotations from the previous
B/C nested roster. Use an a priori hash ordering within each stratum. Annotations were
used in the archived roster qualification only; the executable projection is derived
from string membership in the original short and long histories. These deliberately
enriched development conditions use an unavailable sibling history at deployment time.
They are diagnostic controls and must not be presented as a deployable or leaderboard
method. Do not open sealed tasks.

Freeze before dispatch. At most 24 new Actor and 24 Judge calls. Archive IPE-C/RP
references separately because their generation paths and evidence differ. Estimate
the effect of adding the nested records with and without shared distractors; task-level
actions and OPB, UPB, FM remain separate. Report agreement and behavior of the two
exposure increments, actual protection of B/C, and whether ADDED exceeds both FULL
and the archived specialist. No retries, no relabeling and no modification of old scores.
