# Can reranking saved answers solve the mixed-C tasks?

Start with the 110 exposed C-bearing RPEval tasks having valid IPE-C, task-only and RP endpoints. Add the six archived formal methods (including RP). Preserve the 34 tasks whose eight-way intersection lacks a Match score as missing. On 76 complete tasks and 468 positions, retrospectively taking the highest saved position Match per task gives 310/468; the best single method on this intersection is Des2Gen reconstruction at 287/468. This oracle uses scores and cannot be deployed. Its ceiling applies to these saved candidates and these ratings only.

Only two of 76 tasks have any answer scoring all positions correctly. Both perfect records belong to task-only, for the explicit and implicit versions of the same family outing. They have one source group, one answer hash and one identical Judge reason. The author string is AAAAABC; the reason states that ignoring all preferences satisfies it. The generic half-day itinerary includes a park or zoo and a museum option, so some behavioral alignment is still possible. The stated reason cannot substantiate perfect B/C realization. Original scores remain intact. The JSON reports separate per-method counts; methods, tasks and duplicated answers are not independent draws.

Decision: whole-answer reranking among these existing candidates has limited strict-Exact headroom even under a perfect retrospective selector. Neither of the two perfect records qualifies as independent behavioral evidence for a positive demonstration. A claim about improving actual joint realization needs cases with substantiated action-level contrasts or genuinely new answers. No new model calls or held-out access. Reproduce from the original private research checkout with replay.py.

## A concrete violation in the only perfect answer

The shared family-outing task asks for a city route with a child. The author's B is curiosity about new technology; C rules out any craft activity, including simple DIY. The 7/7 task-only answer actually offers a **craft class** on its rainy-day route. It also recommends a science museum, so mentioning technology does not identify an effect of history here. The explicit RP answer (also the IPE-C answer) proposes interactive science exhibits and explicitly excludes origami, clay and DIY throughout the route. It scores 5/7. For the implicit rendering, RP likewise predicts B/C on the two legal positions and proposes a nature search requiring no crafts; its whole-answer score is also 5/7. The remaining formal methods score 4/7 on the explicit rendering and 4–5/7 on the implicit rendering. All six formal methods mention a science museum in both tracks. No semantic compliance rate is inferred from that string.

The observable failure is a recommended activity in a weather contingency. A system optimizing the recorded exact score could select a demonstrably unsuitable recommendation. The correct deployment implication is to test hard constraints across alternatives (including fallback plans) when comparing future candidates. This one root cannot establish prevalence or a score improvement, and the RP answer may still overuse other memories. verify_outing.py links the author record to the saved route and checks the IPE-C/RP answer identity; OUTING.json contains the original position counts.
