## What changed

- **Yoga studio, `b3684850`.** SELECTED passed only the fact that the requester is mentoring a student opening their own studio. The answer leaves the studio's philosophy and its intended audience open for that founder to choose. The full-history GEPA answer instead emphasizes a traditional shala, making a direction suggested by history central to the new studio. The corresponding ratings are 1 versus 4. The empty-history answer also chooses a broad, decision-supportive framing and receives 1. This is evidence of an advantage over exposing all history after a positive route. It cannot show partial exposure was required for a passing answer.
- **ERP hiring plan, `a42d0679`.** SELECTED, GROUP and EMPTY are literally the same request. Each records 4, as do FULL and RUBRIC. The empty-history answer still favors a phased roadmap. The observed failure cannot be attributed to exposure to candidate history. It also does not establish whether staged hiring itself is erroneous.

Six of the eight risk roots used the same empty-history wire across SELECTED, GROUP and EMPTY. Only two risk paths were physically exposed to selected nonempty histories. Both strict utility roots received nonempty history. The single risk improvement is useful for testing scope, while the six exact shared paths supply little information about fine-grained selection.
