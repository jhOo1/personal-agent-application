The original diagonal gap is ten positions. Keeping the Qwen downstream paths and switching to the GPT activation bit changes two positions. Holding the GPT downstream paths fixed, switching the bit changes none. Keeping the bit fixed while changing the whole downstream path changes eight or ten positions. This is an exact decomposition of saved choices, with an interaction. It does not isolate Actor quality from differences in the full-history RP policy, sampling, or scorer response.
