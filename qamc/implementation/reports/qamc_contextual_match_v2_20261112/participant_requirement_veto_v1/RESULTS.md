The rule changed one RPEval Actor wire and did not change the other 13. On `explicit/047` the independent review extracted the user's explicit requirement against warm lighting and anchored participation in furnishing their current shared home. Restoring that single source reconstructs the original relevance-arm request byte for byte. It reuses the answer that recommends cool light, indirect fixtures and reduced brightness; the parent guard had recommended warm white light instead. Match returns from 3/6 to 4/6, and both UPB and FM severity return from 3 to 0. This is exact endpoint reuse and includes no fresh generation, redraw or rescoring. The other eventual rules and inputs retain the original guard's results.

As a post hoc comparator, restore every source the guard discarded if the saved RP fusion predicted C. The comparator also scores 11/32; it restores both the true C above and one author A on the distinct all-A children's-room task `implicit/142`. It also reuses previous exact requests. Its aggregate OPB is 20 versus 15, and its UPB is 0 versus 4. Those differences both come from the known incompatible-bias evaluation on that single all-A task. Excluding that disputed source, the two protection rules deliver byte-identical answers. The distinction in source admission is observable, while the earlier scores do not establish a reliable performance advantage of the independent reviewer over rank protection. The three author-A removals and one author-C removal were joined to the immutable ranking audit only after generation. The source labels and predictions never enter the independent review.

The four open-decision PersistBench cases retain the original source removals, including the student welcome email, which continues to score 1 compared with ordinary relevance's 4. Project-template and dog-adoption cases still fail. The two utility controls were unchanged before and after both layers, so this follow-up has not demonstrated that the veto protects utility when a relevant source is actually at risk of removal. The two BenchPreS source sets stay at their previous guard outcomes. Both adapted metrics and the original candidate failure decisions stay visible.

10 independent review requests were made, all successful, using **33,356 reported tokens**, zero retries and zero unknown usages. All 14 Actor results and all native grades were reused exactly. Zero-call replay regenerated the candidate request hashes and matched the previous fixed answers. The settlement also validates each physical review record and reconciles the ledger. Data belong to the exposed development set and scores use the frozen local adaptations.

A larger follow-up should freeze different exposed source groups and test participant ties on previously uninspected mixed cases. Both counterexamples matter: a clearly binding restriction on an actual participant and a similarly emphatic past or personal preference that lacks authority over the current shared task. Compare against the same-Actor rules at matched review budget and retain the strongest archived references. Only advance the guard after it repairs an independently observed constraint omission while retaining actual risk reduction. A targeted warm-lighting replay is insufficient for a cross-benchmark claim.
