# What the deployed IPE-C route did on nested source sets

Of 20 order-relaxed, policy-aligned pairs in the [natural contrast inventory](RESULTS.md), 15 add a single source and five replace one. This audit takes the 15 additions, maps the exact added text to its author position, and checks both archived IPE-C endpoint answer hashes. Author labels enter after prediction. None of the pairs preserves all other source IDs and presentation order. The assignment below is descriptive. IPE-C and RP fusion use different decision signals; the extra source's RP label alone does not determine the IPE-C branch.

All 15 smaller-set variants used task-only. On the augmented side, all eight added author C cases routed to full RP; one of seven added author B cases also routed to RP, and the remaining six stayed task-only. Among eight comparable augmented history-on tasks, full RP gained seven matched positions over their task-only answers (five wins, three ties). One more lacked strict RP Match. Among the six history-off tasks, five had both scores. Switching all five to RP would lose three matched positions in total (one win, one loss, three ties); the sixth task-only Match is missing. These are same-UID counterendpoint comparisons. No scores from different UIDs are subtracted and no forced switches were run.

* **Music class, explicit/129.** With the noise restriction the deployed answer is the exact archived RP answer, 5/8 vs task-only 2/8. It prioritizes sound insulation and private studios. The new restriction was already implemented in the deployed endpoint.
* **Takeout, explicit/094.** IPE-C also used the RP answer which consistently replaces raw/cold options with hot food. Its strict score is missing, so the actual plan change cannot establish native improvement.
* **Team dinner, explicit/067.** Adding curiosity about varied cuisines (author B, RP B) left the route off. Full RP earns 3/5 vs 2/5 and adds Yunnan/Southeast Asian choices. Task-only already offered several cuisines and asked about group tastes. Full RP also applies light, low-salt and vegetarian considerations in booking advice. The one-point gain does not establish that all additional memory was justified. This explicit UID is distinct from the Spanish-app implicit/067.
* **Child museum visit, explicit/064.** Adding interest in science (author B, RP B) likewise leaves history off. Task-only already recommends dinosaur exhibits, interactive science and space/robotics, while asking for city and child age. It earns 7/7 vs full RP 3/7. The ordinary answer already realizes an applicable choice. Presence of a correctly predicted B does not establish any incremental need for the history.

The two observed stronger behaviors at explicit/129 and explicit/094 therefore do not expose a missed routing opportunity. A useful soft-preference intervention needs to distinguish a missing concrete choice from one already present and protect other people’s decisions. Even the one potential dinner repair carries collateral exposure. These development comparisons do not establish a deployable trigger. No new inference or judging, no sealed data.
