# Direct Actor demonstration of choice justification

Test one mechanism before transferring it across tasks: explaining an already available option using an applicable remembered feature. Both prompts give a worked design illustration adapted from the exposed Spanish-app task, using the same option, two features and current goal. One connects features to generic task utility; the other supplies past preferences and illustrates their use. These are authored illustrations, not verbatim teacher outputs. Word count and wording differ. No change to the task payload, recovered Qwen IPE-C route, actual history availability, model, cap or adapted GPT-4.1 scoring. Compare matched calls and archived IPE-C separately. The actor receives neither test labels nor scores. Exclude the donor source group. Hash-sample three distinct groups within each frozen history-B, history-C and task-only all-A stratum (nine exposed development cases). Preserve missing scores separately. Compare strict Match, OPB, UPB and FM on common valid tasks. Do not expand without a Match gain over both controls, improvements in at least two distinct groups, no increase in OPB or UPB over the generic illustration, and confirmed improvement in legitimate behavior. Task-only cases check whether an irrelevant illustration contaminates generation. This pilot estimates no independent generalization. Budget 18 new generations plus at most 18 ratings.
