# Message-level discourse compaction

This pilot changes the operation, from editing individual phrases to removing optional whole discourse units. Reuse the eight exposed Strong Gate references from the frozen sentence-simplification study. The roster contains four observed brevity misses, two achieved brevity tasks and two abstention tasks. Both new paths reuse the exact same one-call receipt with the same current task, selected evidence, strong draft and candidate sentences. The first freely rewrites a complete message. The second chooses whole located sentences and only removes them, up to three. Amounts, markup and several modal/conditional forms are screened out of deletion candidates mechanically. The remaining message cannot introduce a changed wording, but omitting an important sentence could still lose context. The selector still judges optionality. Text outside the chosen spans is preserved apart from surrounding whitespace.

Retain existing generation and scoring archives. A task without an applicable visible brevity request under the existing eligibility rule uses its unchanged archived answer. Exact unchanged results reuse scores. Report adapted BenchPreS AAR, MR and completeness, literal length and observed dropped behavior. Because the four known misses share a profile, even a positive pilot requires broader source validation. The primary direction is higher target compliance with unchanged task facts and completeness and no MR increase. An improvement of length alone is not evidence of task quality. No sealed material or new benchmark.
