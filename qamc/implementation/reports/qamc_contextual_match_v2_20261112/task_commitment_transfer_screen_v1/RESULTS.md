## Case and provenance checks

- **RPEval, sleep audio.** The proposed removal was a previous habit of phone scrolling. The Query requests audio to stop scrolling before bed. The history actually describes a morning habit. It does not establish a nighttime alternative the model should follow. The archived IPE-C endpoint had no history and already recommended no-screen audio.
- **BenchPreS, tax correspondence.** The proposal drops a historical news-anchor style. The strong Gate already excludes that preference and addresses the tax agent directly.
- **BenchPreS, benefits update.** Both policies drop a newsletter framing preference. The strong Gate already excludes it and delivers a direct message to the benefits coordinator.

**Protocol wording corrected here.** The archived source evidence used in the screening packets comes from the common reconstructed RP history bank. It is not always the evidence visible to the chosen strong answer. The frozen requests were executed as written by the code. In RPEval the chosen IPE-C endpoint used the full RP packet on five roots and the explicit no-history route on three. Across eight BenchPreS roots the original Gate literally saw only 14 of the 41 screened candidate strings, and none of the six DIRECT proposals targeted a visible source. In PersistBench all 76 candidate strings appear verbatim in the corresponding author-adapted full-history RUBRIC requests. The supplemental audit verifies Actor and answer hashes, understands literal and JSON-escaped source text, and separates exposure from semantic use.

Evaluation-side mapping finds all seven RPEval DIRECT removals at author-A positions with exact M0-indexed alignment. Four were visible to the strong Actor. The six BenchPreS DIRECT removals were labeled inappropriate in the saved task attributes and were already absent. PersistBench does not provide positional A/B/C labels for this comparison. These are diagnostic counts and do not establish terminal gains from ordinary removal. The existing [same-wire RPEval pruning test](../strong_wire_pruning_replay_v1/RESULTS.md) already showed a retained-history result of 6/22 versus the strong draft's 7/22, with no OPB reduction. The current screen provides no reason to expand another one-source hard-removal filter.

**24 physical selector requests, 24 valid responses, 90,035 reported tokens, zero unknown usage and zero retries.** No new Actor or scorer requests, no sealed access, no altered historical grades. The roster is source-separated from the prior PersistBench pilot and still consists of development cases. A paired terminal gain or a replication outside development was not measured.
