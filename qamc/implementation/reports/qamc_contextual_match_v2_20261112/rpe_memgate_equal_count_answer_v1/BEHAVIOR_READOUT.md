# Frozen behavioral checks on the completed Actor outputs

The execution team inspected the predeclared cases after the Actor stage, before using this trial's Judge grades. The conditional comparison below is limited to the six fixed probes and does not replace the benchmark scores. Source/role facts are verified against the unchanged Chinese inputs. No human blind review was run.

| Case | Observed change in the final answer |
| --- | --- |
| Korean for friend's wedding, explicit 057 | EN Gate provides text flashcards, short wedding phrases and audio exercises with an explicit no-video design. Neither alternative recommends video either. The gate increases explicit adherence and remains practical, but the visible difference is weak evidence of a uniquely avoided violation. With one visible item, another author-C record is absent from all three arms. |
| Qingdao family trip, explicit 074 | Only EN Cosine carries the family's no-seafood preference. It recommends home-style cooking, vegetarian dishes, noodles and confirming the restaurant menu. Both gate arms recommend seafood options, including as actual meals, with the English gate also using slow hiking from the requester's unrelated preference. This is a concrete behavior win for cosine. |
| Anniversary dinner, explicit 054 | Chinese gate presents butter scallops, a cream-based main, milk-and-butter mashed potatoes and dairy desserts. Both English arms share the exact output and replace these with a complete dairy-free menu, including a broth-based sauce and coconut-mango sticky rice. This supports language-conditioned selection and does not distinguish the two English rankers. |
| Shared dining room, explicit 134 | The two non-English-gate rankers share a draft that recommends a live low-maintenance plant. EN Gate selects the explicit no-care-requiring-plants record and instead suggests dried or artificial flowers, candles and trays, while retaining practical dining-room ideas. This is a concrete behavior win for the learned ranking relative to the English cosine control on this case. |
| Children's room, implicit 070 | Both English arms share the exact draft. It proposes a fully no-cartoon room, specifying suitable bedding, rugs, wall art and toys. The Chinese-gate draft already avoids proposing cartoon items but does not guard the constraint across the room. This supports explicit realization under the restored source rather than a uniquely avoided mistake in the sampled comparison. |
| Friend's birthday, implicit 083 | Both exact-shared Chinese-gate and cosine drafts make quiet/nature suggestions conditional on the friend's interest. The English-gate draft offers an adventure party conditional on shared taste, with a general fallback. All three still open from the requester's historical interests when choosing a first set of options. We do not label a conditional alternative as a mandatory group default. |

The cases show opposite English-ranker wins on legitimate constraints. Some recorded differences make requirements explicit even when the other answer happens to behave compatibly. Judge readings and disagreements belong in the score report. [Predeclared checks](BEHAVIOR_CHECKS.md)
