## Decision

Do not promote the new prompt. All four soft-task differences versus generic are ties except for a one-position decline on the birthday task. The generic arm's gain of six positions over IPE-C in that stratum comes entirely from the same birthday task; the archived full RP already has 7/8. In both new answers quiet arrangements and light food organize the group celebration. On the parents' anniversary task both new answers suggest making a gift; the feasible arm includes a wooden frame or keepsake box, while generic suggests making a memorial lamp. Both score 2/4. These examples show why graded differences need not establish the proposed mechanism. The candidate also loses one Match position versus generic on the family Chengdu trip. The two all-ignore tasks favor the archived empty-history answers. Comparisons with IPE-C change both exposure and generation; only the two fresh arms share the same exposure and policy. No performance claim on either other benchmark follows from this screen.
