# The remaining opportunity on the strongest archived Actors

The proposed three-arm decision-realization probe does not have enough qualified cases. Two suggested growth examples are upstream misses on the exact strong routes. A separate blind semantic audit of delivered evidence mostly confirms implementation or disagrees with the existing scoring rubric. This audit supports preserving distinct upstream and downstream questions. It does not rank or modify any existing method.

## Decision

Keep the exact IPE-C, Strong Gate and PB_RUBRIC references. A minimum of six RPEval groups and two BenchPreS profiles with verified, already-delivered and unimplemented lawful actions is not supported by this audit. Do not dispatch the proposed Neutral/Requirement/Action generation probe yet. The data support prioritizing retrieval/routing re-entry while protecting all-A and hard constraints, and keeping already-delivered realization as a separate question. The saved re-entry and overlap studies show that simply replacing the old answer or making an existing gate stricter has weak evidence. More concrete opportunities or a materially different control are required.

25 physical requests, 83,157 reported tokens, all transport OK, 17 strict semantic results, no repeats or unknown usage. The transport and result-contract layers are separately reported. Offline replay matches. No Actor, native Judge, sealed cases or benchmark changes. Related diagnostic precedent separates awareness from action, while this audit adds exact delivery and declared policy on existing routes. [Know It, Act on It](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29433)

No previous eligible RP or Gate substitution gives a protected Match gain on those nine richer variants. The reconstruction pins inputs only.
