# Transferring an observed action across unseen source groups

Freeze before reading results. Use the original 296 three-path complete development tasks and the original three source-group folds. For each held-out task, donors come only from the training folds, excluding any matching source group string even across benchmarks. Compare exact matching of the full 12-field estimator summary with nearest matching of the same summary. For the latter take all donors at minimum distance. Distance is mean absolute difference on all fields; convert candidate count to 1/n to keep it bounded and Boolean to 0/1. No learned metric, feature search, domain ID in the decision, or task-specific instructions.

Transfer an action only if every matched donor has a nonnegative change versus its IPE-C path on all recorded axes and at least one donor improves on primary score. Rank eligible actions by their mean within-source primary improvement per position, averaged equally across sources. Break ties by fixed T/H/G order. Otherwise keep IPE-C. This unanimity rule makes the protection decision explicit; it cannot guarantee held-out protection. Exact matching with no donors keeps IPE-C. Deployments would require a library of labeled development outcomes; no neural weights are trained. Archived answer replay only. Compare these rules with IPE-C and the existing strong domain reference; report candidate coverage, changed actions, improved and worsened tasks, and protection losses. Do not tune on the outcome. No sealed material, generation, or new grading.
