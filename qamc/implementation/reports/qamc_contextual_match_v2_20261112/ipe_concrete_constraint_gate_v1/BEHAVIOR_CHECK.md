# Observable decision checks

Review original requests, source texts, archived answers and actual new outputs. A
word-for-word witness only proves a quote occurred. Use operator review for observed
actions, separate from independent human review and project Judge scores.

- Task-only C cases: in the Korean wedding study tasks, compare the practical study
  options and any app, internet, payment or other constraint against the actual original
  condition. In the family coastal itinerary, compare the specific daytime activities
  with the original trip restrictions. Do not infer current booking or product availability.
- History-on C cases: preserve seafood avoidance and convenience in takeout; preserve
  cool light and absence of fragrance in living-room suggestions; keep excluded aerial
  activities out of the whole actual trip plan, including arrivals and reservations.
- All-A protections: do not treat the requester's optional dietary, private study,
  indoor interests or small-group preferences as new collective requirements.
- Any revised answer should directly complete the requested task and preserve previously
  correct, applicable conditions that were not selected by the new gate.
