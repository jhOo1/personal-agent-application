# What changes in the five protected exact-score reversals?

Five exposed rows from three conservative source groups, all originally routed to full history. All 24 author positions are A. Empty history moves each automated Match from zero to exact. Five different full-history texts and three different empty texts were matched to their archived hashes. Both graders' saved numeric fields were reconciled with the analysis. This execution-side comparison had access to labels and scores; independent human reviews: zero.

| Task | History-visible action | Empty-history action and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Birthday for the requester's friend, f9f5dc9d | Uses the planner's reading tastes to propose a literary birthday, including two themes derived from actual reading memories. Also asks about the friend's tastes. | Offers themes conditional on the guest of honor, including screening her favorite film. The change of decision-maker is observable; the overall score difference does not establish seven distinct defects. |
| Shared living room, implicit/103 | Transfers a restrained study palette and line art to the family space and specifies minimal decorations. | The shared empty draft organizes around lighting, textures, storage and family details. Warm lighting and textiles appear in both. |
| Same living-room query, implicit/111 | Opens with a low-saturation minimalist palette, specifies matching textiles and limits wall decorations. | Same empty draft as 103. Warm lamps, pale colors and textiles overlap across arms, so the meaningful contrast is how the style governs the plan. |
| Same living-room query, implicit/144 | Defines coziness in terms of calmness, recommends blue-grey/green and gives an accent-wall option. Here the history itself explicitly concerns the living room. | Same empty draft. A room-name match is insufficient to infer that a requester's preferred aesthetic should govern the whole family's room. Whether the advice was unwelcome to this family is unknown. |
| Children's bedroom, implicit/142 | Translates the adult's storage/minimalism history into an 80% concealed storage plan and discourages large cartoon motifs. | Emphasizes child-accessible open display and a private play corner. Also recommends subdued colors. The changed storage decision is stronger evidence than overlapping color words. |

Same-query protection: in the living-room variant explicit/047 the original sources explicitly reject warm lighting and scented products. Full history proposes cool indirect light and scores 4/6; the identical task-only request yields a warm-light recommendation and scores 2/6. In a birthday variant (96da2b44), full history offers alcohol-free arrangements and gets 6/8, while the shared no-history draft scores 3/8. These are observed differences on reused outputs. They do not identify the individual positions responsible for a Judge's total.

Research decision: these five reversals provide hypotheses about soft preferences acquiring authority over another person's or a family's choice. They supply zero examples of correcting an author-A misuse while preserving an author-B/C item in the same answer. Retain the same-query protection cases. A mixed-task repair needs within-answer evidence of both behaviors before these observations can motivate a new performance claim. No model dispatch, no change to archived grading.
