# Conditional commitment adds no effect over the shared free choice

**Do not promote this compiler.** All eight paired outputs were byte-identical across the free and guarded arms. Three gates validated structurally. In all three the model had already picked the conditional draft. The other five receipts requested no gate. Thus this test identifies no independent effect of committing by the guard. The free choice also saw the shared two-answer proposal contract, so this comparison does not estimate the contract against an ordinary one-answer prompt.

The concrete change is useful evidence that the action contract can elicit a different venue, with no independent compiler increment.

The gate pins vegetarian history and the group request; those anchors do not by themselves verify the low-FODMAP requirement or the adequacy of the proposed substitution. Strict score remains a pass.
- **Coastal itinerary, implicit/148.** The model marked a shopping destination contradicted by history about shopping-heavy free time and noisy parties. Verbatim matching accepted the anchors, but those quotations do not establish a hard prohibition on that destination. The replacement still includes beach and seafood recommendations. No net gain attributable to the guard.
- **Group picnic, implicit/094, and shared dining room, explicit/116.** The picnic retains the two-to-four-person recommendation under both arms, at 0/4. The dining-room answer keeps the original restrictions on wall decorations and fabric, at 4/6.

The program's earliest limiting point is the shared generation's identification of an unsatisfied precondition. A hard rule cannot recover an action that is absent from its own trigger, and verified quotations do not certify a valid trigger. Do not expand this policy or repeat this roster.
