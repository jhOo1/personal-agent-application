# Use the question to decide how much history to expose

If the pre-existing query-only router identifies a specific missing personal input and the selector returns a nonempty set, send full frozen history to the GEPA Actor. Otherwise retain the original selected evidence. The estimator reads only the actual request. It has no benchmark category or score. All assignments reuse the recorded final answer and grade for the chosen exposure. No cloud requests or new grades.

All 93 risk assignments stayed on the original selected path. This preserves every recorded risk answer, including known failures and missing scores. It also means this panel contains no risk test of a wrongly opened full-history path. On fresh utility, 13/18 inputs actually broadened. Two known Qwen failures turned into passes (board-game hosting with the cat allergy, gift for a child with many LEGO sets); one previously unscorable selected answer took a scored full-history path. The fence planting task remained on narrow evidence and still failed Qwen. The change from 14 to 17 observed passes includes only two demonstrated paired wins. Both graders use adaptations; neither sampled independent sources.

A post hoc exposure-matched diagnostic allocated 13 openings uniformly among 18 utility cases and compared only the 17 complete original pairs. Of 8,568 allocations, 7,098 recover at least the observed two known misses. The result supports different exposure rates across task types on this panel; it gives little evidence that the router ranks the most valuable utility openings within type. This diagnostic uses evaluation categories and cannot serve as a deployable comparator. [Counts](EXPOSURE_CONTROL.json)

Retain for comparison on a separately frozen eligible panel with risk tasks that themselves request personal context and utility tasks that do not explicitly ask for it. The saved-input development gain meets the registered screen. No population noninferiority, unified three-benchmark superiority, or official SOTA is established.
