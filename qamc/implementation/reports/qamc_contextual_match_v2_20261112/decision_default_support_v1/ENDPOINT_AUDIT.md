The only protected archive gains are the picnic and children's room, with task-only Match of 4/4 and 5/5 against two zeroes for IPE-C. In both, Strong Gate is also at zero. For the two-person dinner, the task-only answer falls from 3/6 to 2/6 and the prompt gain to 4/6 is flagged. The three protection cases each fall from 4/6 to 2/6 with task-only. These differences require case-sensitive exposure rather than a uniform level of suppression.
