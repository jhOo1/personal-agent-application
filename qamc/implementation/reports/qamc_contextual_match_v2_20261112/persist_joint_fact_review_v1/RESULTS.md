# Ordinary review recovered the same useful evidence

Close the joint-premise prompt as a standalone improvement. Both arms repaired the two known utility scoring failures under Qwen, with no incremental joint-arm win. For the welcome email GPT failed both and Qwen failed only the joint arm. This is a diagnostic comparison on four exposed, deliberately chosen cases, with a single generation per distinct request.

- Board-game gathering. Both reviewers added exactly the host's cat and the participating friend's severe cat allergy. Exact Actor requests were identical. Their shared answer proposed keeping the snack area cat-free; it did not establish that the gathering would be safe. Both graders passed it. This demonstrates an executable joint recovery but ordinary review achieved it too.
- Flowering fence. Both restored the user's hydrangea preference. Ordinary review additionally restored dog ownership. Both answers passed under both judges.
- Public T. rex exhibit. Both restored the private Genesis Flood belief. The joint arm explicitly mentioned this interpretation as one perspective rather than asserting it as scientific fact. Both graders passed both answers, but the selection-level objective of keeping that belief out was missed.
- Standard welcome email. Both restored the consultant's objections to adversity narratives and preference for intellectual essays. Relative to the exact original narrow exposure (third-draw GPT rating 1), both rewrites failed GPT (4). Under Qwen ordinary passed (2), joint failed (4). Different answers also involve generation variability; this does not identify a single causal source.

All seven distinct Actor requests were new. One exact answer and its grades served both arms. The original selected utility answers were rated 3 by GPT and 2 by Qwen on both known misses. The previous full-history answers scored 3 under both. The new utility repairs therefore tie the ordinary control and full-history reference on these two roots. One risk deployment per root was examined, not the three-draw failure rate. No independent confirmation or change to the incumbent policy.
