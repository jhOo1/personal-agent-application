# Retain selective activation as a baseline; bound its advantage

Two fresh deployments on 18 exposed source groups reproduce an advantage over task-only generation. On the 17 roots with paired full-history scores in both deployments, none show a strict IPE-C Match win over full RP in both deployments. Four roots beat task-only in both deployments (18 evaluable for that pair). Route decisions repeat on 17/18 roots. Relative to full RP, routed OPB totals fall in both deployments (34 to 22, 31 to 18) while UPB rises (17 to 32, 15 to 26). In deployment two the missing full-RP Match has an invalid denominator or range. All 216 physical calls completed and their response identifiers were distinct.

The archived endpoints on exactly the 18 assigned cases scored IPE-C 39/91, full RP 39/91, task-only 33/91. The larger archive's lead over full RP was already absent on this subset before redeployment. We cannot attribute the change from the larger panel solely to sampling noise. In the exploratory fixed-opening-count calculation the first routed score (38) is close to the conditional assignment mean (36.67); the second (44) reaches the best available total (mean 36.24). These are post-hoc descriptive controls conditional on the sampled endpoint answers and grades.

## Observed actions on the three predeclared tasks

Arm-masked texts were inspected by the research assistant, with prior knowledge of the task and study. Notes were recorded by answer code before decoding the method. No independent human audit. The checks concern individual actions, not the correctness of every preference position.

Full versus task Match was 4/6 vs 2/6 and 4/6 vs 3/6. | Repeated protection of the eating restriction relative to task-only. | Bedtime listening for a couple, implicit/057 | All four answers explicitly offered quiet piano. Both full-history answers also named solo guitar. | Korean for a wedding, implicit/009 | All four used Korean study materials. Neither full answer prescribed reading English novels or watching English movies. Full RP scored 0/2 then 2/2; task-only scored 2/2 twice. | The first Match win does not demonstrate elimination of the predeclared wrong-material behavior. Keep the larger archival and smaller fresh results side by side. Within these fresh runs selective activation substantially protects hard conditions against empty history and reduces the severity of overuse against full history. Supporting-only cases provide no aggregate Match gain over full RP in either deployment. The results support optimizing the cost of missed legitimate uses on the strong path; they do not establish a uniformly superior method or independent confirmation.
