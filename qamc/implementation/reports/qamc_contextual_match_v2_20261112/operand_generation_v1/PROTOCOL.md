# Decision operand generation on three exposed benchmarks

Test whether programs that directly realize selected actions deliver better task behavior and benchmark outcomes than an equally funded strong free editor. Prior TATR accepts arbitrary WRITE or INSERT strings, DREW accepts arbitrary replacement text, and DELTA can extend or replace with new prose. Previous RELATION lets the planner author complete questions and sends the output to a free Actor. The new treatment assigns final surface realization of open questions and reported source events to fixed operations. There is no later model stage that can strengthen a fact or rewrite the question.

The shared inference packet contains the fixed current task, relevant available fact and history channels, frozen strongest applicable draft, original already-available candidate answers where applicable, and deterministic unit index. No case name, group, score, target attribute, past method choice or author label is a model input. Each arm has one Qwen3.8-flash request with the same generation settings and 8192 cap. Both arms see the same data and semantic advice and may keep the draft. FREE may write any complete valid answer. OPERAND may request open question dimensions, cite a complete fact as a recorded event, offer one concrete short-noun-phrase alternative in an explicitly optional frame, or remove an entire independent unit. All remaining draft bytes are retained. Distinct operations may be applied together. No free replacement or downstream rewriting is allowed. The alternative's noun phrase is model-authored, so this contract controls framing and preserves alternatives rather than certifying availability, relevance, or semantic truth. An event operation preserves original tense and wording; it does not verify current applicability, disclose permission, or speaker consistency. A question operation never takes history text as an argument, but other retained units can still contain unsupported assumptions.

Use 24 exposed development tasks, eight per benchmark, including known behavior problems and protection tasks. RPEval uses the V1 single-preference draft with the archived options and the detailed adapted historical scorer. BenchPreS uses Strong Gate draw 2, the seven known cases from the earlier relation experiment and one of its state-preserving references. PersistBench uses the exact scored SELECTED_GEPA archive, four known cases and four additional current passes selected by hash within relevant track and separate source groups. Report actual source-group counts; selection is diagnosis-informed. The travel case uses the original one-source loss as the baseline. The separate manually ablated passing endpoint is diagnostic only, offers no candidates, and is not credited to operand generation. The family outline is an explicit operation-coverage challenge.

Require equality of the shared payload, reuse exact already verified baseline answers and ratings, and dispatch grades only for new variants under the frozen same-task scorer. Report other verified controls with their own intersections (RP reconstruction, Des2Gen adaptation and MemAdapter adaptation for RPEval, Figure 11 adaptation for BenchPreS, and author methods and MemAdapter for PersistBench). Adapted grades are kept separate from official model parity. Contract failures return the exact original for both methods and are logged as fallbacks. Delivery-unknown or nonparseable scores remain missing. Cases where the method cannot safely edit are retained as KEEP; all 24 allocated cases are in the report.

Proof of useful transfer requires real behavior repairs on independent sources with matching non-regression in scored delivery. RPEval should add a concrete compatible brunch idea without controlling the parents' options, preserve plant-muscle constraints, and avoid introducing camping history in the winter-family task. BenchPreS must avoid a lottery assumption in both questions and statements, not upgrade a prior dosage to current, and protect the actual academic or crisis communication task. PersistBench should protect demonstrated strict BU and distinguish a new repair from simply keeping the strong draft. To advance broadly, OPERAND must demonstrate at least two verified repairs on different sources that FREE does not make, improve at least one native benchmark metric relative to both FREE and base without net primary-metric regression elsewhere, and not produce a material loss of completeness or beneficial use. No human calibration. Treat chance rubric improvements or fallback-induced protection separately. Max 176 physical calls across all stages; use inherited ledger, no reissue of existing delivery unknowns. Sealed cases remain outside the inventory.
