Neither gives a verified gain on the original benchmark.

The ranked answer discusses grammar and travel phrases, with no comparable shadowing practice located. The archived IPE-C and rank Match scores are missing. The agreement answer is more explicit about teammates swapping roles. Both and IPE-C are 4/5 with scoring flags. The scoped experiment identifies a difference in specificity, not an otherwise missing practice category.
- **Colleague dinner.** IPE-C only mentions atmosphere in a conditional phrase; the agreement recommendation lists concrete ambience features and the ranked answer also proposes quiet seating without the focal source. All three score 5/7. The ambience choice remains subordinate to the task and documented restrictions; the read does not prove a better shared default.
- **Family trips and music.** All three answers receive concrete credit on the remaining four focal records. The earlier loss in Xi'an and Qingdao persists at the whole-answer level, so a positive focal soft-use finding is not enough to justify promotion.
