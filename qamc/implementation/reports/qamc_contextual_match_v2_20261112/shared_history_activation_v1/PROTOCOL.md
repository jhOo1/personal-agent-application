# Shared history activation on two exposed benchmarks

Freeze 18 distinct RPEval source groups and 18 PersistBench groups. Use the six smallest salted UID hashes with unique source groups within each stratum in the code's stated priority order. RPEval has paired task-only and full RP archived endpoints. PersistBench uses the archived full/empty equal-cap GEPA endpoints, including previously missing deployments. No new Actor or Judge. Eligibility is specific to the archived RPEval complete endpoint pool and PersistBench fixed 49-group panel. These panels were previously studied.

A fresh ordinary applicability instruction and the decision-change instruction receive exactly the same current task, facts, and original histories. One binary model call per arm per task. Neither sees the task category, archive scores, other answers or UID. Same rule across benchmarks; endpoint Actors and scoring protocols remain benchmark-specific. Openings expose the entire archived history, with the archived RP full policy retained on RPEval. Training-free and no example retrieval. This is a test of an operational decision rule, not a new estimator of a causal effect.

Primary contrasts are decision against ordinary and the respective archived IPE-C and SELECTED methods. Also report fixed endpoints, Strong Gate for RPEval and RUBRIC for PersistBench. RPEval uses paired Match, OPB, UPB and FM, reporting all-A, only-B and contains-C separately. PersistBench uses risk failure on any of the three deployments, and one strict beneficial-use test. Missing labels, unknown deliveries and unscored endpoints stay missing; show assigned and jointly scored values. One selection controls all repeated endpoint draws. SELECTED and GROUP retain their own archived deployment-specific selections. Do not call a local development win SOTA. Require positive RPEval Match versus ordinary, no lower only-B or contains-C Match, a strictly lower risk-failure count on at least one PersistBench track without higher failures on the other, and no fewer verified beneficial passes on common roots. Also report differences to the strongest archived comparators. No sealing, method switching by benchmark, or score-based case replacements.
