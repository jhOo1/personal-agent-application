# Scope of the routing collision

This retrospective diagnostic covers all 49 exposed roots in the matched evidence comparison (12 sycophancy, 19 cross-domain, 18 strict utility). It compares the same GEPA generator with the exact selected subset vs the whole pool when selection is nonempty. With zero selected sources both paths are identical. Inputs with different exposures also have separately sampled answers. Utility uses the fresh 18-root deployment, where all select at least one source. The comparison uses all 31 risk roots for three draws each. Each risk draw is scored individually here; the original three-draw per-source endpoint remains in its own report.

On the first available draw of each source, neither judge has any confirmed narrow-only pass. The 49-root result alone thus gives no example of opposite pass preferences within the same router/count cell. Counting all 111 deployments reveals two collisions on distinct source pairs. In the grounding/two cell Qwen favors group on planting (utility draw 1) and narrow on the public exhibit (risk draw 3). GPT favors narrow on the exhibit and ties on planting. In grounding/one GPT favors group on the family-relationships chapter (risk draw 1) and narrow on the student letter (risk draw 3). Qwen ties on the chapter and favors narrow on the letter. The latter pair lies within the same sycophancy track: knowing the track would not resolve this instance. Switching a single deployment in the chapter does not imply the whole root passed the three-draw criterion.

Each judge has one confirmed pair of opposite preferences in one cell. The two judges do not corroborate both sides of either pair. Under each grader any deterministic lookup of branch and count must forgo at least one observed passing choice relative to picking the better stored answer per deployment. This lower bound is conditional on observed draws, ties, missingness and pass thresholds. It does not estimate a population-level irreducible error rate. In particular, zero-count ties are structural; they cannot validate safe suppression of all memory.

Retain task routing as a comparator. These sparse collisions motivate work on discriminating the contents of competing sources, but do not justify training a larger router on this panel. Compare any next candidate against the original direct generation with multiple risk deployments and strict utility; report both graders and the complete assigned denominators.
