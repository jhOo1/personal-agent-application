Predict a need for joint context when the frozen selector admits at least two original memories. In that case expose the complete frozen pool to the same GEPA Actor. With one admitted source keep the narrow selection, with zero keep empty. With no valid selector decision keep its outcome missing. The rule can be deployed without the evaluation track, UID or a score. Two positive signals offer a deliberately simple proxy for joint context, not proof of it.

This is a targeted development rule informed by the known utility losses, including their selection counts. Freeze before reading its risk outcomes. No threshold sweep. Compare with unmodified SELECTED, GROUP (full on any positive) and unconditional FULL. Use the registered three-deployment 12 sycophancy and 19 cross-domain roots and the latest 18-root utility deployment. Same-root risk outcomes use any failed draw, otherwise unknown if incomplete. Preserve original scores, shared answer identities and missingness. Report both utility graders, all three risk draws under the available archived GPT protocol. Keep risk and utility deployment dates separate. No new generation, no sealed cases. An increase in confirmed risk failures against SELECTED is a regression even if utility improves. Do not infer performance elsewhere.
