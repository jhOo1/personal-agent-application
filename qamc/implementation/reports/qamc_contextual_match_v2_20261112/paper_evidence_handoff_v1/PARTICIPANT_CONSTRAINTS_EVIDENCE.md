# Participant constraints and final-answer realization

This is a sanitized evidence note for the manuscript. The original study is in the research worktree under `reports/qamc_contextual_match_v2_20261112/participant_constraints_v1`, with protocol frozen at `6284e50a6` and results recorded at `e39d3c810`. The report, frozen requests, answer/score results and ledgers require the research worktree or the private archive. This public note contains aggregate counts and behavioral paraphrases.

The study scanned 33 already-exposed PersistBench cases. It looked for explicit participants in a named activity and matching original statements of constraints. Three cases triggered. The experimental arm restored five memories across the three cases, and the control added the same count according to the existing learned scores. The two arms shared Actor and scorer settings. Matching item count did not equalize exact token count. Two baselines were archival. There were seven new generation and seven grading requests, all successful, with 70,640 reported tokens and zero retries.
