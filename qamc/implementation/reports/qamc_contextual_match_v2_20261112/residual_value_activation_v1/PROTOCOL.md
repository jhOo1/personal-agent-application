# Residual-value override of the full-history route

An intermediate task-only answer gives both reviewers a concrete comparison point before choosing whether to deliver the full-history answer. Reviewers do not see the full-history answer, benchmark labels, IDs, scores or scoring rationales. The residual reviewer looks for an actual task gap that a source could fill. Generic and residual arms share the exact original task, sources and saved task-only response, and each uses one equally configured call. The method uses history only when IPE-C would originally have enabled it, and can override to task-only. If the new decision is invalid or failed, keep the original IPE-C answer and record the fault. Source-span checking confirms reference integrity, with no guarantee of semantic correctness. The clause asking for a residual is the change under test; it does not introduce additional retrieval or a repair stage.

Freeze four all-A, five B-only and five C-containing originally history-on previously exposed roots, disjoint by source group and excluding the six design groups. Sort by fixed hash and select without reading endpoint scores; availability and byte identity of the original answers are required. The labels only stratify the experiment offline. Reuse both stored scored Actor paths without dispatching either Actor or a Judge. Both arms incur the cost of producing the task-only draft and a routing call in a new deployment, plus the original IPE-C computation and the activated full-history Actor on the history-on branch. Report the archive replay as such.

Primary metric is exact paired Match under the project scoring protocol. Preserve assignment, flags, missing scores and invalid reviewer replies. Compare to IPE-C and generic routing on joint valid endpoints. Consider expanding only if there are actual changes on at least two source roots with credible paired gains and no net loss against either comparator, no aggregate rise in OPB, UPB or FM against IPE-C, and no observed loss of a binding constraint moved to the task-only path. This pilot may establish diagnostic opportunity only. Models and datasets outside this panel remain fixed.
