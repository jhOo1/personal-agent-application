## Behavior and attribution

- **Audio to fall asleep with a partner.** RESIDUAL used the existing task-only answer, while DIRECT kept history. Match moved from 0/7 to 7/7, OPB from 5 to 0. Both saved answers offer several overlapping kinds of audio, including environmental sound, calm stories, and guided relaxation. The task-only answer leads with a practice that both partners can follow and offers concrete search terms. Such differences do not explain every position-level judge change. This is a clean automatic-grade win, with no independent confirmation of seven separate changes in preference use.
- **Learning an instrument in a dorm, `implicit/021`.** The residual quote about watching instrument construction and technique videos was valid. The suggested use prioritized resources on how instruments work. It did not establish that the current user needed that priority for a practical dorm-friendly beginner recommendation. The selected history-on answer opens by invoking the user's drumming preference and leads with practice pads/electronic drums. Ordinary review chose the task-only endpoint, which starts with ukulele and kalimba and gives concrete beginner resources. Match was 0/5 for RESIDUAL and 5/5 for DIRECT. Quote grounding does not certify residual utility.
- **A friend's birthday, `explicit/055`.** RESIDUAL switched to task-only. The full-history answer explicitly invokes the user's preferences to propose venues; the task-only answer gives alternatives and lets the choice depend on the recipient and event. Both score 2/5 with the same bias fields. This observed behavioral change did not yield an original-metric gain and does not establish a second promotion-eligible win.
- **Zhangjiajie, `explicit/056`.** The residual rationale identified an actual participant's restriction on high-altitude activities. It kept history as intended. The chosen endpoint was the original strong answer, so retaining access is not evidence that its final actions all satisfied the condition.

The frozen roster contains four all-A, five B-only and five C-containing roots from different exposed source groups, excluding the six design groups. Both arms read identical task-only drafts and fixed candidate memories. This control isolates the change in routing instructions, not the value of materializing an intermediate draft. A new deployment would incur its generation cost. The replay offers a reason to pursue actual recipient relevance, with the observed failure of treating any specific useful-looking action as enough. Do not add these two routers after seeing their complementary wins and present that replay as a prospectively validated method.
