Both ROUTED and EMPTY fail in both waves, and FULL moves from 2 to 4, despite qualifiers appearing in routed and full outlines. Preserve the concrete differences and the scoring uncertainty.

## Coverage, cost and scope

All 114 logical answers are scored. There are 250 physical requests (38 route, 38 select, 87 Actor, 87 Judge), all successful with reported usage, no retries, and 1,792,925 tokens. The other 54 logical requests from the maximum of 304 are eliminated by in-wave deduplication, 27 Actor and 27 Judge. The request ledger confirms the same 8,192 cap on every physical Actor request. Each wave draws fresh physical responses. Previous raw scores and sealed cases were untouched.

The resulting paper evidence supports a tradeoff: activated history can be useful relative to zero history on BU tasks, and zero-history exposure explains the observed safety gains over full history on the risk panel. A single policy's independent generalization, complete utility preservation, an additional Style contribution, author Kimi protocol results, and external SOTA remain unestablished.
