# Speech-act ordering for BenchPreS delivery

The generation contract expresses the message as purpose, context, and optional follow-up, plus any appropriate subject, greeting and signoff. The renderer delivers the purpose first, followed by substantive context and follow-up, with earlier and final envelope text as generated. This enforces order and prevents duplicate paragraphs and internal structure labels. It does not verify correspondence of generated facts or completeness. The mechanism changes the generation interface and rendering, so a benefit cannot be assigned to ordering alone.

Reuse all 12 exposed cases from the first-pass delivery panel and their archived Direct and Content-first controls. These include nine same-input non-bold misses selected by the earlier fixed census and three protection cases. The only request change relative to Direct is its system suffix. The task, original selected evidence, Qwen alias, cap, temperature, and Qwen attribute/completeness grader are identical. Existing answers and scores remain read-only and can be reused for exact scoring requests. Same-budget controls were generated in an earlier period.

Count malformed JSON or render-invalid answers as missing; preserve the receipt and do not regenerate. Budget twelve Actor calls and up to 72 score calls. Compare actual AAR, non-bold AAR, MR, completeness and tangible delivery facts, always on matched valid cases. Consider expansion only if at least two profiles show incremental non-bold realization over both Direct and Gate without an increase in inappropriate preference use or decrease in task completeness. The panel is exposed and failure-enriched; results are not independent and do not reproduce the author R1 scoring protocol. No sealed data, gold inputs at inference, or retries.
