## Decisions across stages

- **Two routing misses.** The Korean wedding draft from full history provided a no-registration study path and kept recording local. The selected empty-history answer did not establish that constraint, although its spoken-language lesson was useful. Both archived Match scores were 1/3. The empty-history reason notices the omitted C; the full-history reason credits it while discussing unrelated positions. In the Qingdao family plan, full history offered a concrete personal alternative to shopping. Empty history promoted souvenir shopping without a comparable personal alternative. Match was 5/7 vs 3/7, but full history has a grading flag and also gives other interests an effect on the shared itinerary. A corrective switch needs to recover the personal option without making shopping avoidance a family-wide rule.
- **Visibility and execution.** In the family restaurant task, empty history makes private rooms and Chinese cuisines the default. The IPE-C draft recommends an open dining room and non-Chinese cuisine, improving Match 2/8 to 5/8. In the picnic task, empty history selects a grassy outdoor plan, while IPE-C uses indoor nearby board games to accommodate pollen and an outdoor aversion. Both examples have two C requirements that can be realized together. In the French course task, a self-study itinerary replaces tutor sessions at unchanged 4/6 Match. This is a visible C improvement even though other positions can offset it.
- **One execution conflict persists with history visible.** For healthy takeout, both endpoints recommend fish. IPE-C explicitly excludes seafood ingredients in one section but recommends fish elsewhere, including its weekly plan. Its grader credits the seafood constraint and fails to address the conflicting recommendations. This has no existing numeric consistency flag. The same draft does recover ready-to-eat options, so deleting the whole answer or clearing memory would discard useful content. The appropriate unit of verification is the recommended meal or ingredient wherever it appears in the actual plan, extending beyond a single explanatory paragraph.
- **Shared settings require context.** In the new-home dining room, IPE-C uses lighting and low-maintenance decor, while empty history recommends a living potted plant. Both receive 2/6. The stored history-side reason assigns the plant preference to Ignore although the authored role is C. The choice does not prove what the partner wants. In contrast, both endpoints already give parents usable, thoughtful anniversary gifts without recipient-side assembly in the primary physical options. That remains a protection case.

The review supports two separable directions. To improve routing, investigate why an applicable hard memory loses the C trigger and where restoring it would change a specific decision. To improve generation, focus on a genuine remaining contradiction after the evidence has reached the Actor, with a control that sees the same draft, source and task. The two failure mechanisms require different interventions. No new method is promoted by this audit. Behavior judgments are execution-side and sometimes uncertain, such as actual app access, crowd levels, or whose family preferences apply. All original preferences, endpoints, and benchmark grades remain frozen.
